> 
> It comes from the attention stack, per an independent breakdown of the released config. The layers split 3 to 1: 34 layers of Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), the cheap linear-attention kind, and 11 layers of Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) with DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA), which do the expensive exact work. It also carries a DeepSeek V4 style residual path, four parallel streams running through the block instead of one, plus a native vision encoder.
> 
> That split is unusual because both halves are already efficiency mechanisms. Kimi pairs its KDA layers with layers that still run full attention. DeepSeek V3.2 pairs DSA with full-attention MLA. This one pairs two cheap mechanisms and spends its entire full-attention budget on 11 of 45 layers.
> 
> The other two releases in the window do a different job. Qwen3.8-Flash is the cheap, high-volume row: 125B total, 6B activated per token plus 51B of N-gram embeddings, at $0.15 per 1M input tokens, $0.47 per 1M output and $0.016 on cache hits. The 6B pays for the $0.15, since serving cost tracks what runs per token rather than what sits in the checkpoint. On the one benchmark both labs report, DeepSWE v1.1, Alibaba puts it at 58.7 against GLM-5.3-Flash's 63.4, so a third of the active parameters costs you about 5 points on a self-reported score. Also, check its context figure before you plan around it, because Alibaba published two. The Aug 26 announcement says 262K native, extensible to 1M, while the Aug 28 post says 1M outright.
> 
> DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp is narrower still, a vision variant of the text model DeepSeek already serves rather than a new flagship: the lab says it holds V4-Flash's text performance on agents, reasoning and world knowledge, and adds a jump on multimodal agent benchmarks that brings that performance close to Claude Opus 4.8.
> 
> Overall, which row sits on top depends on the column you sort by, and the mistake is sorting by the biggest headline benchmark number. The columns that decide are active parameters, context, price per million, and which benchmark the lab picked for itself.
